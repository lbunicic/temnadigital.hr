English Hrvatski
Your reflections are yours.

Sentir does not track you, show ads, sell personal information, or send your journal to Temna Digital.

Who we are

Sentir: Daily Examen is provided by Temna Digital d.o.o., Ulica Dore Pfanove 4, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia. Contact us at info@temnadigital.hr.

Information stored by the app

Sentir stores the reflections, Examen entries, habits, preferences, and reminder settings you choose to create. By default, this information stays on your device. Temna Digital cannot read it.

Optional iCloud sync

If you enable iCloud sync, Apple stores your Sentir data in your private CloudKit database under your Apple Account so it can stay in sync across your devices. Reflection text and other sensitive journal fields use encrypted CloudKit fields. Temna Digital does not operate a server that receives this data and cannot access your private CloudKit database.

Apple Watch, widgets, and reminders

Sentir shares only the minimum state needed with its Apple Watch app and widgets through Apple platform services and local app-group storage. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. We do not receive your notification schedule or Watch activity.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Temna Digital does not receive your payment-card details. Apple may provide the app with purchase status needed to unlock features.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Sentir contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Support

If you choose to contact support, your email and anything you include in your message are used only to respond to your request. Sending support email is optional and happens through your chosen mail service.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete individual entries in the app. Removing the app deletes its local data, subject to device backups. If iCloud sync is enabled, you can manage app data through your Apple Account and iCloud settings. You may contact us with a privacy question at any time.

Children

Sentir is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because we do not operate user accounts or collect journal data, we do not knowingly collect such information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Sentir or applicable requirements change. The current version and effective date will remain available on this page.

Podaci koje aplikacija sprema

Neobavezna iCloud sinkronizacija

Ako uključite iCloud sinkronizaciju, Apple sprema Sentir podatke u vašu privatnu CloudKit bazu povezanu s vašim Apple računom radi sinkronizacije između uređaja. Tekst refleksija i druga osjetljiva polja koriste šifrirana CloudKit polja. Temna Digital nema poslužitelj koji prima te podatke i nema pristup vašoj privatnoj CloudKit bazi.

Apple Watch, widgeti i podsjetnici

Sentir s Apple Watch aplikacijom i widgetima dijeli samo najmanju potrebnu količinu podataka putem Appleovih usluga i lokalne app-group pohrane. Podsjetnici se zakazuju na vašem uređaju. Ne primamo vaš raspored obavijesti ni podatke o korištenju Watcha.

Kupnje

Kupnje unutar aplikacije obrađuje Apple putem StoreKita. Temna Digital ne prima podatke o vašoj platnoj kartici. Apple aplikaciji može poslati status kupnje potreban za otključavanje značajki.

Analitika, oglašavanje i praćenje

Sentir ne sadrži oglasni SDK, SDK za analitiku trećih strana ni praćenje između aplikacija. Ne prodajemo niti iznajmljujemo osobne podatke.
